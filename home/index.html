Lean Past 40 — Recomposition Coaching

No metabolic magic. A moderate calorie deficit, progressive strength work, and a deliberate return to maintenance — with your coach on the numbers every week. Every claim on this card is something you can see in your own training log.

1 · Base Camp — weeks 1–12

Strength programming matched to your decade (40s, 50s, and 60s tracks) plus calorie & protein targets built from your own numbers. Moderate deficit only — no crash dieting, no “detox.”

2 · The Ascent — weeks 13–24

Loads climb while calories walk back up to maintenance on a schedule. Recomposition instead of rebound — most clients finish eating more than they started because muscle and training raised what their body needs.

3 · The Summit — ongoing

You run it yourself: a maintenance plan, quarterly check-ins, and you keep the app — your programs, logging, progress charts, and the nutrition guide.

Questions

Frequently asked

How does online coaching work?

After your free assessment, you’ll get a personalized plan in your client portal with video-guided exercises, weekly programming, and scheduled check-ins. You train on your schedule; I adjust based on your feedback and progress.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. Programs are tailored to whatever you have — full gym, home setup, or minimal equipment. We build around your environment.

What ages and levels do you work with?

Everyone from youth athletes and parents to recreational competitors and PGA-level golfers. Programs scale to any starting point.

Can you help me return from an injury?

Yes — that’s a core specialty. With a PTA and manual-therapy background, I build progressive, safe return-to-play phases. (Note: coaching complements, and does not replace, medical care.)

What’s your cancellation policy?

Month-to-month, cancel anytime before your next billing date. Sessions can be rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance.
